**Table S7. Stretches of the hand-corrected route geometry that buses do not follow (35 routes, January–August 2024).**

Each route's path is cut into 100 m stretches and compared, month by month, with the raw position reports: a stretch is *skipped* when buses cross it without being seen (they run along another street), *wrong way* when most reports near it head the other way, *offset* when the path is drawn to one side of where buses run, and *rarely travelled* when far fewer buses pass than on the rest of the route. Listed are runs of consecutive stretches with a problem in at least 3 of the 8 months, and temporary detours reviewed by hand. Persistent stretches add up to 14.0 km of 1,484 km of path (0.94%); by route, 0.0–7.4% (median 0.5%), and 11 routes have none. Temporary detours: 4.0 km on route 101.
